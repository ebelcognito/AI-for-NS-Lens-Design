## What this benchmark actually measures

Ranked by how much they discriminate:

1. **Does the model unroll the folded path into a sequential surface list?** CODE V
   models this class *sequentially*: the surface list names one physical face more than
   once — here 6 surfaces from 4 faces (A, D, B, A, T, B). A model that reaches for a
   non-sequential / NSC-style construct has misread the tool. This is a known attractor
   and should be scored even if the file loads.
2. **Reflected-space sign handling.** After each reflection the propagation sense flips,
   so thicknesses alternate sign down the chain (or the model places surfaces globally
   and sidesteps this — also correct, see below).
3. **Cylinders, not spheres.** The design is purely meridional; a spherical A/B face is
   wrong physics, not a style choice. Watch for this specifically — it is the single
   easiest thing to get wrong while producing a file that looks right.
4. **Reflections that stay in glass.** The four TIR events must not be modelled as
   refractions, and must not silently become coated mirrors (there are no coatings).
5. **Face identity.** A appears twice and B appears twice. Two independent copies of a
   face is a real defect: it decouples the design and breaks any later optimisation.
6. **Tilt handedness.** Face normals are given as vectors, not as tilt angles, so the
   model must convert — and pick a sign. Magnitudes right / signs wrong is the classic
   failure and is what scattered `fold_cell_4_11.folded.seq` into a star.

## Two legitimate encodings

Both are acceptable; the file must pick one and be internally consistent.

**(a) Global placement.** Every surface placed by its absolute vertex + tilt in one frame
(decenter-and-return with zero axial thickness between them). Robust — no sign chain.

| # | face | vertex (z, y) | face tilt (°) | radius (mm) | event |
|---|------|---------------|---------------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | A | (47.939893, 0.0) | 25.000000 | 156.054833 | refract into glass |
| 2 | D | (47.166609, +30.0) | 90.0 | flat | reflect, stay in glass |
| 3 | B | (75.254482, 0.0) | 15.638246 | 197.543133 | reflect, stay in glass |
| 4 | A | (47.939893, 0.0) | 25.000000 | 156.054833 | reflect, stay in glass |
| 5 | T | (69.553580, −30.0) | 90.0 | flat | reflect, stay in glass |
| 6 | B | (75.254482, 0.0) | 15.638246 | 197.543133 | refract into air |
| 7 | detector | z = 120.0 | 0 | flat | — |

Tilt = angle of the face's local +z (its base-plane normal) from global +z, toward +y.
Radius *magnitudes* are exact; the CODE V sign depends on which side the ray arrives
from and is checked via the trace, not by matching a sign in this table (see caveat).

**(b) Folded axis / local chain.** Real thicknesses between consecutive hit points, each
fold a reflective surface, thickness sign flipping after each reflection:

| surface | face | event | thickness to next (mm) | local tilt (°) | radius (mm) |
|---------|------|-------|------------------------|----------------|-------------|
| stop | — | offset y = +9.459225, semi-ap 4.0 | 37.2073 | field angle 23.8848 | — |
| 1 | A | refract | 11.8446 | 10.8762 | 156.054833 |
| 2 | D | TIR | −28.4868 | 27.5345 | flat |
| 3 | B | TIR | 39.5413 | 41.8930 | 197.543133 |
| 4 | A | TIR | −17.1783 | −37.8684 | 156.054833 |
| 5 | T | TIR | 13.1297 | −35.5838 | flat |
| 6 | B | refract | 63.9225 | −26.7830 | 197.543133 |
| 7 | detector | — | — | — | flat |

Local tilt = half the deflection at a fold, the incidence angle at a refraction. This is
what `export_fold_folded.py` emits (`fold_cell_4_11.folded.seq`).

## Scoring — 12 points

| # | Check | Pts | How to verify |
|---|-------|-----|---------------|
| 1 | File loads without a syntax error | 1 | open in CODE V |
| 2 | Built **sequentially** — no non-sequential construct attempted | 1 | inspect (known attractor) |
| 3 | 6 surfaces from 4 faces, in the order A, D, B, A, T, B | 1 | surface list |
| 4 | A and B are **cylindrical**, axes out of plane; D and T flat | 2 | surface types |
| 5 | Four TIR events are reflections that stay in glass; the two refractions cross the boundary the right way; no coatings invented | 2 | surface properties |
| 6 | Global positions of all surfaces match the table to <0.01 mm / <0.01° | 2 | global-coordinate listing |
| 7 | Repeated faces genuinely coupled — edit A's radius, both move | 1 | edit and re-list |
| 8 | Collimated 23.8848° input with the offset stop at z = 0, semi-ap 4 mm | 1 | system data |
| 9 | Central ray lands at (120, +27.55) ± 0.05 mm | 1 | real ray trace |

**Partial-credit signals worth logging separately** — they discriminate better than the
total: (a) tilt magnitudes right / signs wrong; (b) spheres used where cylinders are
required; (c) the four TIRs modelled as mirrors rather than as reflections at an
uncoated interface; (d) face A or B silently split into two independent surfaces;
(e) the reflected-space thickness signs not alternating in encoding (b); (f) a
self-reported trailing table that disagrees with the file's own surface data.

## Caveats — read before grading

- **There is no verified-good reference `.seq`.** Our own exports are unvalidated in
  CODE V: `fold_cell_4_11.folded.seq` imports but scatters the surfaces into a star, and
  the two exporters disagree on radius sign (`faces_of` gives −156.0548, the folded
  emitter +156.0548). So grade checks 1–8 by inspection against the *geometry*, which is
  validated, and treat check 9 as requiring an actual CODE V run. Do not fail a model for
  a radius or tilt sign that our own files also get wrong — score handedness only where
  the trace proves it.
- Check 9 is the only one that needs the keyboard. Everything else is gradable from the
  file text plus this key.
- **Harder variant:** delete the "Verified reference trace" section from the prompt. The
  model then has to trace six circle/line intersections and Snell refractions by hand at
  0.001 mm precision with no tools. Expect near-total failure — useful only once the
  standard variant saturates.
